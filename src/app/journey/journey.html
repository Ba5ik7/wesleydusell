This is Wesley DuSell’s tribute to 21 years of web development. The first era follows the creative promise of Flash and the shift toward an open, mobile web.

The dates describe overlapping movements, not a strict succession of inventions. WordPress began in 2003; AJAX named existing techniques in 2005; jQuery launched in 2006. Flex Builder became Flash Builder in 2010. ColdFusion and SOAP continued beyond this era.
